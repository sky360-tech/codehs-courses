Give a short description of each of the following license 
types in your own words.


1. CC BY
It is the most open license. It allows the user to redistribute, to create derivatives, such as a translation, and even use the publication for commercial activities,

2. CC BY-SA
The Creative Commons Attribution Share-Alike license allows re-distribution and re-use of a licensed work on the conditions that the creator is appropriately credited.
    

3. CC BY-ND
CC-BY-ND or Creative Commons Attribution NoDerivs is a more restrictive license. It allows you to redistribute the material commercially or non-commercially but the user cannot make any changes whatsoever to the original

4. CC BY-NC
It is a Creative Commons license which a copyright holder can apply to their media to give public permission for anyone to reuse that media only for noncommercial activities.

5. CC BY-NC-SA 
This license lets others remix, tweak, and build upon your work non-commercially, as long as they credit you and license their new creations under the identical terms.


6. CC BY-NC-ND
It is the most restrictive license offered by Creative Commons. With this license, the user (while attributing the original creator) can only share the work but not change it in any way or ever use it commercially.

Of the six licenses, which type is the most restrictive in terms of how you can use the work?  
I think CC BY ND is the most restrictive.

Which type is the least restrictive? 

CC BY is the least restrictive.
