Algorithm: The School Morning Routine
Step 1: Wake up and make the bed.

Step 2: [REPETITION] * Check time: Is it before 7:15 AM?

Action: If yes, stay in bed and browse your phone.

Condition: Repeat this check every 2 minutes until the clock reaches 7:15 AM.

Step 3: [SELECTION]

Check the weather: Look out the window.

Decision: * If it is raining, put on a raincoat and boots.

Else, put on a standard school hoodie and sneakers.

Step 4: Walk to the kitchen and find breakfast.

Step 5: [REPETITION]

Action: Take a bite of cereal and chew.

Condition: Repeat until the bowl is empty.

Step 6: [SELECTION]

Check the bag: Do I have my math homework?

Decision:

If "Yes," walk to the front door.

Else (If "No"), go back to the desk, grab the homework, and then walk to the door.

Step 7: Leave the house and head to the bus stop.

Step 8: [REPETITION]

Action: Stand at the bus stop and look down the road.

Condition: Repeat until the bus arrives.

Step 9: Board the bus and arrive at school.
